01About Me

I am not trying to build technology that makes people more connected.

The internet was built to close distance between people. What got built on top of it optimised for feed ranking, notification timing and infinite scroll, all tuned to time on platform rather than time well spent. A follower count is not a friendship. A contact list is not a community.

The open question now is whether artificial intelligence finishes what social platforms started which is another automated layer between people, dressed up as connection.

I realized how it can do the opposite.

AI should not write the messages that matter or manage the relationships that matter. It shouldn’t draft your apologies, automate your check-ins, or simulate empathy to a friend in need.

Let humans keep our humanity.

Ai should take the part that was never really a relationship to begin with. The moderation queue, the spam filter, the verification email, the reminder that fires on schedule whether or not a person is the one sending it.

I am trying to build technology that makes people less busy performing connection, earning more time to actually connect with those who matter.

05Research

Quantum Inspired Agentic Test Case Prioritisation

MPhil thesis · Universiti Teknologi Malaysia · In progress

A data independent framework for prioritising regression tests, aimed at the cold start problem: what do you run first when you have no execution history to learn from?

  • Uses large language models for semantic analysis of code changes, extracting fault risk and complexity features on the fly rather than from historical data.
  • Ranks tests through a classical quantum inspired depolarising channel model, sidestepping the computational cost of metaheuristic search such as PSO.
  • Runs on an automated GitHub Actions and Google Apps Script pipeline that pulls code diffs by webhook and triggers Python evaluation scripts.
  • Supported by a systematic literature review on the scalability and effectiveness trade offs of AI driven prioritisation.

  • Regression testing
  • Systematic review
