    02About Me

    No platform I have used has ever admitted this out loud: it needs your attention more than it needs your relationships.

    The internet was built to close distance between people. What got built on top of it optimized for something else: feed ranking, notification timing, infinite scroll, all tuned to time on platform rather than time well spent.

    A follower count is not a friendship. A contact list is not a community. We got very good at collecting connections and worse at tending to the ones already in front of us.

    Now the question is whether artificial intelligence finishes what social platforms started: one more automated layer between people, dressed up as connection.

    I think it can do the opposite.

    AI should not write the messages that matter or manage the relationships that matter. It should take the part that was never really a relationship to begin with: the moderation queue, the spam filter, the verification email, the reminder that fires on schedule whether or not a person is the one sending it.

    None of that is glamorous work, but it is not nothing either. It is the hours that quietly disappear from a day, hours a person could spend on the two or three relationships that actually need them instead of the two or three thousand that do not.

    I am not trying to build technology that makes people more connected.

    I am trying to build technology that makes people less busy performing connection, so there is time left to actually practice it.

    04My Best Work

    Telegram Community Project

    Administrator · UTM Confession

    What started as a semi-anonymous university confession channel grew into a community platform serving more than 12,000 subscribers.

    I built and operated the automation behind it: Google Forms submissions, anonymized Telegram publishing, automated moderation, spam detection, scheduled notifications, and university-email verification.

    The system handles the repetitive administrative work on its own, so the community stays active without anyone manually processing every submission or request.

    Beyond the confession channel, I built a gated community for student entrepreneurs and clubs, where verified students can promote their activities through an automated approval workflow.

    The platform has also generated over 2,000 Telegram Stars through customised digital usernames.
